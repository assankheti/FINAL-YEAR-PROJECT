# 9. User Manual

## 9.1 Introduction

### 9.1.1 System Overview

Assan Kheti is a comprehensive digital agricultural platform designed to empower farmers and agricultural professionals in Pakistan by providing intelligent farming solutions. The system integrates modern technology with agricultural expertise to deliver real-time insights, market connectivity, and knowledge resources tailored to the Pakistani farming community.

### 9.1.2 Purpose of the Application

The Assan Kheti application serves as a unified platform that addresses key challenges faced by farmers:

- **Disease Detection & Management**: Real-time crop disease identification using AI-powered image recognition
- **Market Connectivity**: Direct farmer-to-consumer marketplace for agricultural products
- **Agricultural Intelligence**: Smart budget planning, fertilizer recommendations, and pest management solutions
- **Community Engagement**: Knowledge sharing and direct farmer-to-farmer communication
- **Comprehensive Resources**: Seed information, fertilizer databases, and agricultural calculator tools

### 9.1.3 Target Users

The system is designed for multiple user categories:

1. **Farmers (Primary Users)**
   - Small to medium-scale agricultural producers
   - Users seeking market access and crop management tools
   - Individuals requiring agricultural guidance and disease detection

2. **Buyers/Consumers**
   - Individuals purchasing agricultural products directly from farmers
   - Organizations seeking bulk agricultural supplies
   - Users browsing community marketplace offerings

3. **Agricultural Community Members**
   - Professionals sharing agricultural knowledge
   - Experienced farmers providing guidance
   - Agricultural experts participating in community groups

## 9.2 System Access

### 9.2.1 Login and Registration Process

#### Registration for New Users

**Step 1: Launch the Application**
- Open the Assan Kheti mobile application on your device
- If this is your first time, you will be presented with the splash screen

**Step 2: Language Selection** [Screenshot Here]
- Select your preferred language from the available options:
  - **English** (Global interface language)
  - **Urdu** (صارف کے لیے اردو میں انٹرفیس) - Recommended for Urdu-speaking users
- Your selection will be saved for future sessions
- Note: The application automatically detects your device language but allows manual override

**Step 3: User Type Selection** [Screenshot Here]
- Choose your role in the system:
  - **Farmer**: Select this if you are an agricultural producer selling products or seeking farming guidance
  - **Buyer**: Select this if you are purchasing agricultural products or browsing marketplace listings
- This selection determines the interface layout and available features
- You can modify this selection later in your account settings

**Step 4: Phone Number Verification** [Screenshot Here]
- Enter your mobile phone number in the designated field
- Include your country code (e.g., +92 for Pakistan)
- Tap "Send OTP" button
- An SMS with a One-Time Password (OTP) will be sent to your registered number within 2-3 minutes

**Step 5: OTP Verification** [Screenshot Here]
- Enter the 6-digit OTP received on your phone
- If you did not receive the OTP:
  - Wait 2-3 minutes before requesting a new one
  - Ensure your phone number is correct and SMS is enabled
  - Tap "Resend OTP" to request another code
- Tap "Verify" to proceed

**Step 6: Profile Setup** [Screenshot Here]
- Enter your full name
- Add a profile picture (optional but recommended for trust-building)
- For Farmers: Enter your agricultural area/region
- For Buyers: Enter your delivery address
- Tap "Complete Registration"

**Successful Registration Message**: "Your account has been created successfully. Welcome to Assan Kheti!"

#### Login for Existing Users

**Step 1: Open the Application**
- Launch the Assan Kheti app
- If you are already registered, the app will display the login screen

**Step 2: Enter Credentials** [Screenshot Here]
- Enter your registered phone number
- Tap "Send OTP"

**Step 3: Verify OTP**
- Enter the 6-digit code sent to your phone
- Tap "Login"

**Step 4: Home Screen**
- After successful authentication, you will be directed to your personalized dashboard

## 9.3 Installation Completion and First-Time Setup

### 9.3.1 Initial Steps After Installation

#### Permissions Configuration (First Launch)

When you first launch the Assan Kheti application, the system will request the following permissions:

**1. Camera Access** [Permission Dialog Here]
- **Required For**: Disease detection feature (uploading crop images)
- **Action**: Tap "Allow" to enable camera functionality
- **Impact**: Without camera access, disease detection features will not work

**2. Photo Library/Gallery Access**
- **Required For**: Uploading product images and profile pictures
- **Action**: Tap "Allow" to access stored images on your device
- **Impact**: Limited functionality if denied; you can still take photos directly

**3. Location Access (Optional)**
- **Required For**: Determining weather conditions for your region
- **Action**: Tap "Allow Once" or "Always Allow"
- **Impact**: Provides location-based weather and fertilizer recommendations

**4. Notification Permissions**
- **Required For**: Receiving alerts about orders, messages, and important system notifications
- **Action**: Tap "Allow"
- **Impact**: Without notifications, you may miss important updates

**Note**: All permissions can be modified later in your device Settings > Apps > Assan Kheti

#### Initial Profile Completion

After permissions are configured:

1. **Review Your Profile Information**
   - Tap Settings > Profile
   - Verify all details are accurate
   - Add a professional profile picture

2. **Complete Required Information**
   - **For Farmers**: 
     - Your agricultural area/district
     - Types of crops you grow
     - Farm size (optional)
   - **For Buyers**:
     - Delivery address
     - Preferred product categories

3. **Enable Notifications**
   - Go to Settings > Notifications
   - Toggle "Enable All Notifications"
   - Configure notification preferences (push, SMS, in-app)

4. **Set Language Preferences**
   - Go to Settings > Language
   - Select your preferred interface language (English or Urdu)
   - Toggle "Voice Guidance" if you prefer audio-assisted navigation (for Urdu and English users)

### 9.3.2 Required Configurations

#### Network Configuration

- **Internet Connection**: Ensure you have a stable 3G/4G or WiFi connection
- **App Data Usage**: Assan Kheti uses approximately 50-100 MB per month for normal usage
- **Initial Sync**: First launch may take 30-60 seconds to download necessary data

#### Account Security

1. **Enable Two-Factor Authentication (Optional but Recommended)**
   - Settings > Security > Enable 2FA
   - Verify via SMS OTP on each login from a new device

2. **Update Emergency Contact**
   - Settings > Profile > Emergency Contact
   - Add a trusted contact number for account recovery

#### Regional and Operational Preferences

1. **Set Your Primary Crop Type** (For Farmers)
   - Go to Settings > Farming Preferences
   - Select your main crops from the list
   - This customizes recommendations and alerts

2. **Configure Marketplace Preferences** (For Both Users)
   - Settings > Preferences
   - Set minimum/maximum prices for notifications
   - Enable alerts for specific product categories

## 9.5 Step-by-Step User Guides

### 9.5.1 Guide for Farmers: Adding Products to Marketplace

**Objective**: List your agricultural products for sale to potential buyers

**Prerequisites**: Farmer account with verified phone number

**Step-by-Step Process**:

**Step 1: Navigate to Product Management**
- From the Home Dashboard, locate the "My Products" section or tap the Marketplace tab
- Tap "Add New Product" or the "+" button
- Confirm you are adding a product for sale (not searching)

**Step 2: Enter Product Details** [Screenshot Here]
- **Product Name**: Enter the name of your product (e.g., "Basmati Rice - Grade A")
- **Category**: Select from dropdown:
  - Crops (Rice, Wheat, Cotton, Sugarcane, etc.)
  - Vegetables (Tomato, Onion, Potato, etc.)
  - Fruits (Mango, Orange, etc.)
  - Dairy Products
  - Other Agricultural Products
- **Description**: Provide 1-2 sentences describing your product
  - Example: "Premium basmati rice from Kalarkahar region, freshly harvested"

**Step 3: Add Product Images** [Screenshot Here]
- Tap "Add Photos"
- Select "Take Photo" (use camera) or "Choose from Gallery"
- Upload 2-4 high-quality images showing:
  - Full product view
  - Close-up details
  - Packaging (if applicable)
- Arrange photos in desired order by dragging

**Step 4: Set Pricing**
- **Price per Unit**: Enter price in Pakistani Rupees (Rs.)
- **Minimum Order Quantity**: Specify minimum units to purchase
  - Example: "50 kg" or "1 bundle"
- **Available Quantity**: Total units you have for sale
- **Unit Type**: Select (Kg, Bundle, Piece, Liter, etc.)

**Step 5: Add Location and Availability** [Screenshot Here]
- **Pickup Location**: Your farm address or distribution point
- **Delivery Available**: Toggle ON if you can deliver products
- **Delivery Range**: Specify delivery area (e.g., "Within Lahore" or "All Pakistan")
- **Shipping Cost**: Set cost for delivery (Rs.)
- **Availability**: Set when product is available for pickup/delivery

**Step 6: Add Product Specifications**
- **Harvest Date**: When was the product produced/harvested?
- **Storage Conditions**: Describe how product is stored
- **Certifications** (if any): Organic, pesticide-free, etc.
- **Contact Method for Inquiries**: Phone, WhatsApp, In-App only

**Step 7: Review and Publish** [Screenshot Here]
- Review all entered information
- Verify images are clear and accurate
- Tap "Publish Listing"
- **Success Message**: "Your product has been listed successfully! Buyers can now see your listing."

**Your product is now visible on the marketplace for 30 days**

**Post-Publication Actions**:
- **Manage Listings**: Edit, pause, or delete products from "My Products"
- **View Inquiries**: See buyer questions and respond promptly
- **Update Availability**: Modify stock quantity as items sell
- **Renew Listing**: Re-post after 30 days to maintain visibility

### 9.5.3 Guide for Using Disease Detection

**Objective**: Identify crop diseases using AI-powered image analysis

**Prerequisites**: Farmer account, camera access enabled

**Step-by-Step Process**:

**Step 1: Open Disease Detection**
- From Home Dashboard, tap "Disease Detection" card
- Or: Marketplace tab > Disease Detection option

**Step 2: Capture or Upload Crop Image** [Screenshot Here]
- **Option A - Take Photo**:
  - Tap "Capture Photo" / Camera icon
  - Frame the affected crop area
  - Ensure good lighting
  - Tap shutter button
  
- **Option B - Upload Existing Photo**:
  - Tap "Upload from Gallery"
  - Select a previously saved image
  - Ensure image shows affected plant area clearly

**Best Practices for Image Capture**:
- Focus on diseased leaves or affected areas
- Ensure adequate lighting (midday is best)
- Include scale reference (coin, hand) for size perspective
- Avoid blurry or dark images
- Take multiple angles if possible

**Step 3: Analyze Image**
- System processes image (typically 5-15 seconds)
- Shows loading indicator
- AI model analyzes for diseases

**Step 4: View Disease Detection Results** [Screenshot Here]
- **If Disease Detected**:
  - Disease name and scientific classification
  - Confidence level (e.g., "92% confidence")
  - Severity assessment (Mild, Moderate, Severe)
  - Affected area highlight with visual overlay
  
- **If No Disease Detected**:
  - "Plant appears healthy" message
  - Tips for preventive care

**Step 5: Get Treatment Recommendations**
- View suggested remedies:
  - Organic solutions
  - Chemical treatments
  - Preventive measures
  - Recommended fertilizers or pesticides
- Each recommendation includes:
  - Description
  - Application method
  - Dosage/quantity
  - Expected results timeline
  - Associated costs (if available)

**Step 6: Access Related Resources**
- Tap "Learn More" to read detailed articles
- Link to fertilizer/pesticide recommendations
- Community forum discussions on this disease
- Expert contact options

**Step 7: Save and Share Results**
- **Save**: Results are automatically saved to "Detection History"
- **Share**: Export analysis as PDF or share with farm advisor
- **Track**: Monitor disease progression over time using multiple scans

## 9.8 Troubleshooting Guide

### 9.8.1 Common Problems and Solutions

#### Problem 1: Cannot Log In / OTP Not Received

**Symptoms**:
- "Invalid OTP" error after entering code
- "OTP not received" after 5 minutes
- Phone number not recognized

**Possible Causes**:
- Incorrect phone number entered
- SMS network issue
- Phone number not registered in system
- Device time not synchronized

**Solutions**:

1. **Verify Phone Number**:
   - Ensure number includes country code (+92 for Pakistan)
   - Check for typos (e.g., +92300... vs +923000...)
   - Verify phone is SMS-enabled

2. **Request New OTP**:
   - Tap "Resend OTP"
   - Wait 2-3 minutes for SMS to arrive
   - Check spam/junk messages folder
   - If still not received, wait 1 hour before trying again

3. **Synchronize Device Time**:
   - Settings > Date & Time
   - Enable "Automatic date & time"
   - Restart application

4. **Use Alternative Phone Number**:
   - If available, try registering with different verified number
   - Contact support if account locked

#### Problem 2: Application Crashes or Freezes

**Symptoms**:
- App closes suddenly
- Screen becomes unresponsive
- Functionality stops working

**Possible Causes**:
- Insufficient device memory
- Old application version
- Corrupted cache data
- Background app conflicts

**Solutions**:

1. **Clear Application Cache**:
   - Settings > Apps > Assan Kheti > Storage > Clear Cache
   - Do NOT tap "Clear Data" (this deletes account info)
   - Restart application

2. **Update Application**:
   - Open App Store (iOS) or Play Store (Android)
   - Search "Assan Kheti"
   - If update available, tap "Update"
   - Restart device after update

3. **Free Device Memory**:
   - Close background applications
   - Delete unnecessary photos/videos
   - Restart your phone
   - Ensure at least 500 MB free storage

4. **Reinstall Application** (Last Resort):
   - Uninstall Assan Kheti
   - Restart device
   - Reinstall from App Store/Play Store
   - Log in with your account (data saved in cloud)

#### Problem 3: Images Not Uploading / Camera Not Working

**Symptoms**:
- "Camera access denied" message
- Image upload fails
- Product photos won't attach

**Possible Causes**:
- Camera permission not granted
- Image file too large
- Poor network connection
- Device storage full

**Solutions**:

1. **Enable Camera Permission**:
   - Settings > Apps > Assan Kheti > Permissions > Camera
   - Ensure "Allow" is selected
   - Restart application

2. **Reduce Image File Size**:
   - Use device built-in photo editor to compress
   - Resize images before upload (max 5 MB per image)
   - Use JPEG format instead of PNG (when possible)

3. **Check Internet Connection**:
   - Ensure WiFi or mobile data is connected
   - Restart WiFi router or switch to mobile data
   - Try uploading on different network

4. **Free Device Storage**:
   - Delete old photos/videos from device
   - Clear app cache (not data)
   - Ensure at least 1 GB free storage

#### Problem 4: Orders Not Appearing / Messages Not Sending

**Symptoms**:
- Placed order not showing in Orders tab
- Messages marked as "pending" indefinitely
- Cannot see notifications

**Possible Causes**:
- Synchronization issue
- Network connectivity problem
- Server overload
- Account permissions issue

**Solutions**:

1. **Refresh Application**:
   - Pull down from top of screen to refresh
   - Or: Close app completely and reopen
   - Wait 30 seconds for data to sync

2. **Check Internet Connection**:
   - Verify connected to WiFi or mobile data
   - Switch between WiFi and mobile data
   - Restart your device

3. **Clear Cache and Refresh**:
   - Settings > Apps > Assan Kheti > Storage > Clear Cache
   - Reopen application
   - Log out and log back in

4. **Try Again Later**:
   - If server overloaded, wait 30 minutes
   - Retry operation
   - Contact support if issue persists

#### Problem 5: Payment Issues / Transaction Declined

**Symptoms**:
- "Payment failed" error
- Fund deducted but order not confirmed
- Cannot add payment method

**Possible Causes**:
- Insufficient balance
- Card/wallet authentication failed
- Network interruption during payment
- Account restrictions

**Solutions**:

1. **Verify Payment Method Balance**:
   - Check your bank account/wallet balance
   - Ensure sufficient funds for transaction
   - Verify card/payment method not expired

2. **Try Different Payment Method**:
   - Use alternate card or wallet
   - Try Cash on Delivery option (if available)
   - Contact farmer directly for alternative arrangements

3. **Check Transaction History**:
   - Settings > Payment History
   - If fund was deducted but order not confirmed:
     - Wait 24-48 hours (may process delayed)
     - Contact support with transaction reference

4. **Enable Two-Factor Authentication**:
   - Some cards require additional verification
   - Complete any bank authentication requests
   - Retry payment

#### Problem 6: Disease Detection Giving Incorrect Results

**Symptoms**:
- Detection identifies wrong disease
- "No disease found" but plant clearly sick
- Confidence level very low (< 50%)

**Possible Causes**:
- Poor image quality
- Disease not in model database
- Multiple diseases present
- Image of pest damage (not disease)

**Solutions**:

1. **Retake Image**:
   - Ensure good lighting (midday/sunlight)
   - Focus on affected area clearly
   - Avoid shadows and reflections
   - Take multiple angles if possible

2. **Consult Second Opinion**:
   - Ask in Community Groups for expert advice
   - Share analysis with agricultural extension officer
   - Contact farm advisor for verification

3. **Review Similar Diseases**:
   - If confidence low, review "Similar Conditions"
   - Read descriptions of similar diseases
   - Compare symptoms with your plant

4. **Contact Expert Support**:
   - In-app support > Disease Detection Help
   - Share image with agricultural specialist
   - Get professional diagnosis

A: Currently, Assan Kheti supports:
- English (Global interface)

A: Yes. We use:
- End-to-end encryption for messages
- Secure OTP-based authentication
- SSL/TLS for all data transmission
- No sharing of data with third parties
- Regular security audits
See Privacy Policy for complete details: Settings > About > Privacy Policy

**Q4: Can I change my user type (Farmer/Buyer) after registration?**
A: Yes. Go to Settings > Profile > Account Type and select your new role. You can toggle between roles, though maintaining accurate information helps build trust in the community.

**Q5: How do I delete my account?**
A: To permanently delete your account:
- Settings > Account Settings > Delete Account
- Confirm deletion (this cannot be undone)
- All data will be removed within 30 days
- Existing orders will be canceled

- Archive listing for future use

- Deliver on time
- Maintain product quality
- Respond to messages quickly
- Be honest in descriptions
- Resolve customer issues promptly

A: Currently, discounts must be negotiated directly with buyers through messaging. Future updates may include built-in discount/coupon features.

**Q4: Do I need to verify my farmer account?**
A: Verification is optional but highly recommended:
- Increases buyer confidence
- Boosts search visibility
- Improves credibility
- May lead to more orders
Submit documents in Settings > Profile > Verify Account

**Q5: What if a buyer doesn't pay for their order?**
A: 
- If using Cash on Delivery: Refuse delivery and contact support
- If using digital payment: Case automatically escalated to admin
- Assan Kheti team will investigate and resolve
- Repeat offenders face account restrictions
